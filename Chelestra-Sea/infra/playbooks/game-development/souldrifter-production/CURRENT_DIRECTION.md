## Narrative direction and delegated revision authority

- The current creative target is an identifiable **unofficial fan continuation of The Death Gate Cycle**, not generic fantasy with the source names erased. Book-history, places, character names and events should recur meaningfully across quests, dialogue, objects, journals, map descriptions and the broader storyline.
- Source references must be researched. Distinguish verified book history, owner continuation premises, approved project additions, agent-revised drafts, inferences and in-world rumors. Do not present a new SoulDrifter event or timeline fork as published source canon.
- Assigned content workers may autonomously revise draft quest wording, objectives, motivations, prerequisite links and local roles to fit the lore and advance the campaign, within the current scope and owner locks. Preserve IDs/history, validate the graph/saves and send a batched revision digest; do not ask permission for every small change.
- Final owner content review still applies to a coherent revised pass. Major retcons, a source character's new fate/direct cameo, unsupported mechanics, new peoples in Heartvale, changed budgets/economy and irreversible save/ID changes require escalation.
- These instructions supersede older blanket anonymization and draft-by-draft preapproval wording for the development lane. They do not authorize copying book passages, claiming official endorsement, provider spending, public/commercial release, merge or deployment. Rights status remains separate and must not be assumed from POC/noncommercial use.
- The overarching campaign builds personal power, recovered knowledge, alliances, stability, infrastructure/access and resources/authority to face the possible return of ancient powers. Preserve separate realm identities. Starter content does not acquire late-game powers merely by referring to their history.

## Current gameplay/production constraints

- Real-time combat is the default; turn-based is optional over the same authoritative simulation.
- Tripo is the primary new asset generation/segmentation/retopo/rigging/baseline-animation lane; Mixamo is legacy/fallback under the applicable asset policy.
- Playable characters use modular base bodies, faces, hair, accessories, gear, weapons and runtime assembly.
- NPCs may be generated fully clothed and segmented; new #510 Human cast remains distinct from approved temporary QA fixtures.
- Monsters are regenerated/compared or retained only after the applicable current-harness review. Valid mesh-only testing does not approve rejected motion or final production art.
- Current content covers First Breach and the Heartvale early chapter; use live #442/#498 progression decisions rather than the old Levels 1–9 snapshot. Current Heartvale planning targets approximately levels 2–10 after First Breach.
- Heartvale's local social population is Human-only. Historical references to other peoples/distant book places are permitted; physical nonhuman cameos are not implicitly authorized. Player eligibility and developer test-avatar appearance are separate contracts.
- Late Sartan/Patryn/Labyrinth/rune/possibility systems remain deferred as gameplay systems; source-backed historical references and foreshadowing are encouraged.
- Summoner starter magical creature is the Lesser Driftling, followed later by Minor and Major Driftlings.
- Summoner uses a separate pet bar with manual and per-ability autocast, pet Mana and Summoner Command.
- Every starter class targets three active abilities; at least two form a setup/opening/payoff relationship.
- Each class has its own named resource and individual skill cooldowns.
- Follow current #501/#509/#512 work claims and local evidence, not old issue titles. Preserve checkpoints, accepted work and ownership; no duplicate environment workers on the same files.
- Workers stop at IMPLEMENTED_UNVERIFIED; independent verification and final owner review remain separate from autonomous draft editing.
