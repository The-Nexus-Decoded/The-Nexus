# SoulDrifter Current Direction Snapshot

- Real-time combat is the default; turn-based is optional over the same authoritative simulation.
- Tripo is the primary new asset generation/segmentation/retopo/rigging/baseline-animation lane; Mixamo is legacy/fallback.
- Playable characters use modular base bodies, faces, hair, accessories, gear, weapons, and runtime assembly.
- NPCs may be generated fully clothed and segmented.
- Monsters are regenerated/compared or retained only after new-harness QA.
- Current content scope is First Breach / Heartvale / Levels 1–9.
- Late Sartan/Patryn/Labyrinth/rune/possibility systems are deferred.
- Summoner starter magical creature is the Lesser Driftling, followed later by Minor and Major Driftlings.
- Summoner uses a separate pet bar with manual and per-ability autocast, pet Mana, and Summoner Command.
- Every starter class targets three active abilities; at least two form a setup/opening/payoff relationship.
- Each class has its own named resource and individual skill cooldowns.
- Workers stop at IMPLEMENTED_UNVERIFIED; independent verification is required before OWNER_READY.
