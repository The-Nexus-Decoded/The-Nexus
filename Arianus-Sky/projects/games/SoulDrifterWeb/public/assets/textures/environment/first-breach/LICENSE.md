# First Breach environment textures

The browser-sized PBR maps in this folder are sourced from ambientCG and are
released under CC0 1.0.

| Local prefix | ambientCG asset | Source | Download variant |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `flagstone-*` | Tiles083 | https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Tiles083 | 1K JPG |
| `masonry-*` | Bricks102 | https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Bricks102 | 1K JPG |

Included channels are color, OpenGL normal, roughness, and ambient occlusion.
The source height maps are intentionally omitted from the browser build because
the authored room geometry supplies its own physical depth and silhouette.
