# Chronicle Visual Continuity Bible

Every Chronicle illustration must be checked against this sheet before it enters the game. A recurring figure, animal, object, costume state, rune language, or realm cannot be redesigned from page to page merely because a generation prompt changed.

## Haplo

- Tall, lean, sinewy Patryn man around thirty; square cleft chin, firm mouth, crystal-blue eyes, and short stubble.
- Short-to-medium brown hair with visibly whitening tips. Never bald and never given unrelated hair colors.
- The Patryn rune system begins at the heart/name sigla and branches as connected red-and-blue chains across the chest, stomach, back, arms, legs, backs of hands, tops of feet, and neck. It is not decorative fabric print.
- Leave substantial readable skin between chains. Patryn runes do not cover the head, face, fingers, palms, or soles.
- **Concealed among mensch:** long charcoal/gray travel layers hide the body. Cloth wraps or bandages cover neck, wrists, and hands. Only tiny accidental rune edges may show; no glow when he is not casting.
- **Casting or aboard his rune-ship:** enough skin may be exposed to read the heart-centered system. Inactive runes remain muted red and blue. Only the pathways currently carrying magic brighten in those same colors.
- Uses the same angular face, blue eyes, white-tipped brown hair, build, travel palette, and rune geometry on every page.

## Haplo's dog

- The same medium-sized, nondescript black dog in every scene.
- Lean, practical Labyrinth-survivor build; slightly wolfish silhouette, upright/semi-upright ears, dark brown eyes.
- No breed changes, colored patches, collars of different colors, size changes, or fantasy glow unless the story explicitly depicts soul transfer.

## Lord Xar

- Older, powerful Patryn with the same family physiology as Haplo.
- Mostly white hair with darker brown retained nearer the roots; weathered stubble; severe features and strong survivor's build.
- An older, expanded heart-centered rune system crosses his exposed body, but still leaves skin between linked sigla and never covers his face or scalp. During active gate work, only connected red-and-blue channels glow.
- His visual identity is dominance, age, accumulated scar tissue, and layers of overwritten runes—not a crown or conventional royal armor.

## Alfred

- Older Sartan; long white hair and beard with darkening tips where visible, softer build, worn pale robes.
- Sartan magic appears as sung/danced luminous sigla shaped in the air. Do not give Alfred Patryn body tattoos.
- His face, robe layers, hair, and warm gold-white spell color remain stable between pages.

## Rune-state language

1. Inactive Patryn runes: muted red-and-blue linked sigla with clear skin between chains.
2. Alert/danger response: faint localized red-and-blue pulse.
3. Active casting: a readable connected chain illuminates from the heart/name area through the body to hand, weapon, surface, or ship.
4. Permanent object structure: dark engraved runes remain visible; only the working circuit glows.
5. Sartan casting: voice, gesture, and ordered gold-white signs in the air, not Patryn-style body circuits.

Never place Patryn tattoos on the head, face, fingers, palms, or soles. Favor interlaced squares and octagons joined by flowing curves rather than dense generic tribal coverage.

## Rune growth by age and power

- **Child:** the heart/name rune and a few short protection links. Most skin is unmarked.
- **Adolescent or new survivor:** a developing chest circle with sparse branches toward shoulders, neck, and limbs.
- **Adult Patryn:** a functional connected network across the prescribed body regions, with generous visible skin between chains.
- **Veteran such as Haplo:** a mature, readable system with more protective and survival chains, but never uniform full-body fill.
- **Lord Xar:** the most extensive network in the Chronicle. His age, power, and repeated additions may create layered or partially overwritten sigla, yet the system still respects the untattooed face, scalp, fingers, palms, and soles.

Crowd scenes must vary rune density by age, experience, and magical strength. Never clone Xar's coverage onto every adult or give children completed veteran circuits.

## Labyrinth and Nexus

- The Labyrinth is a sentient prison-world of distinct forests, thornlands, ruins, caves, ravines, hostile chambers, settlements, fortifications, gates, wastelands, and occasional localized maze passages.
- Never depict the entire Labyrinth as a uniform hedge maze or repeating stone grid.
- Patryn refuges and moving survivor bands should make the prison feel inhabited across generations.
- The Nexus is the ordered paradise beyond the Final Gate and must remain visually distinct from the hostile territories.

## Review gate

Before accepting an image, compare it side-by-side with every earlier page containing the same subject. Check face, age, hair, skin tone, clothing state, rune placement and glow, dog coat/build, equipment, ship silhouette, realm palette, and scale. A visually strong image still fails if continuity breaks.
