## Authoring contract

The twenty actions in this pack are original whole-body Blender keyframes made
from `human-foundation-pilot-runtime-4k.glb`, the accepted textured rest rig.
That GLB has SHA-256
`B86F7378ADA29FF11E0FBC030D438FE241B8D4A74C47AFD37CC8ACED28C5FF81`,
65 bones, root `mixamorig:Hips`, and zero imported actions.

No animation library, rejected candidate pack, or previously authored action is
loaded or sampled while the actions are built. Source-clip sampling, reversal,
mirroring, splicing, overlays, pose copying, and relabeling are forbidden. The
left/right actions are independently evaluated pose functions; neither side is
generated by reflecting the other. Temporary hand IK is only an authoring aid:
its evaluated result is baked to ordinary bone keys, and every temporary
constraint and target is removed before export.

The footage below is biomechanics reference, not animation data. No frames,
poses, transforms, timing curves, or motion capture are imported from it.

## Validation contract

The builder performs two factory-reset imports. First it reimports the exported
pack and compares normalized-time bone translation, rotation, and scale
trajectories to the authored actions. It then factory-resets again, imports the
accepted textured zero-action rig, imports the action pack separately, removes
the pack skeleton, and plays every action on the accepted rig.

The second pass verifies precise evaluated skinned-mesh contact and grounding,
root-motion direction, average root speed, and loop closure. Airborne motion is
not forced to the floor. Water Dive is grounded only during the approach;
Neutral Land To Run is grounded only after contact; stairs use their authored
ascending or descending contact surface. The generated JSON report is the
machine-readable evidence for every action and remains `UNREVIEWED` until the
BREACH-V2 owner review is complete.
