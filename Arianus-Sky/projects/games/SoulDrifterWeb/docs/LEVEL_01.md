# Level 01 — The First Breach

## Player-facing goal

Awaken at the shattered Soul Well, recover the first sigil, defeat the Hollow Sentinel, and free the trapped Soul Essence.

## Spaces

| Space | Purpose | Interactions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soul Well chamber | Arrival, atmosphere, movement tutorial | inspect Well, read environmental feedback |
| Runebound alcove | Reward exploration | open coffer, gain the Sigil of Anchoring |
| Ember corridor | Establish animated environment | torches, pillars, training remains |
| Sealed arena | Prove both combat schedulers | Sentinel encounter, active block, action bar |
| Essence focus | Resolution and next-world hook | release Soul Essence |

## Current controls

- Left click a walkable floor tile to move.
- Click a nearby object to interact; clicking a distant object approaches it first.
- Choose **Tactical Turns** or **Real-Time Action Bar** before or during the demonstration encounter.
- Use **Rune Slash** for the primary attack.
- Use **Anchor Guard** to reduce incoming damage.
- Press the reaction prompt or `Space` to actively block when offered.

## Acceptance criteria

- The page loads without requiring downloaded Ultima data.
- The entire map and every visible asset are generated by this repository.
- The character navigates around blocked tiles.
- The coffer grants the starter sigil once.
- Entering the arena begins combat in the selected style.
- Both modes use the same health, damage, guard, and victory model.
- Defeating the Sentinel reveals/releases Soul Essence and completes the level objective.
- No combat action requires reagents or uses the UO spell system.
