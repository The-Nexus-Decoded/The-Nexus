# Tripo Human Body Source Briefs

Status: locked source-image briefs for SoulDrifter issue #487
Scope: eight realistic adult Human foundation bodies, each producing one canonical body mesh and two required pose artifacts

## Non-negotiable production contract

These briefs create source images for animated, modular game bodies. They do not create calling gear, finished characters, portraits, statues, or promotional art.

- Generate exactly eight Human body identities: masculine and feminine presentations across Slim, Medium/Natural, Athletic/Muscular, and Thick/Large-Framed builds.
- Generate exactly one complete adult Human in each source image. The source is one front-facing, full-body, strict symmetrical T-pose, not a multi-view sheet and not a scene.
- Every source is realistic rather than cartoon, chibi, toy-like, miniature, or exaggerated World-of-Warcraft-style art.
- Every source is bald or has a uniform neutral very close scalp that reads as flat skin texture, with no hairstyle, beard, moustache, facial hair, or raised hair geometry. It wears only modest, close-fitting, flat-seam, opaque matte neutral-gray underwear; that required underwear is the sole permitted material touching the body.
- The entire head, both arms, both open hands, every separated finger, both legs, both feet, and the complete silhouette must be visible and uncropped with empty margin on every side. Preserve clean negative space at both armpits and between both legs; no limb may touch or fuse into the torso, another limb, or any body contour.
- Use an orthographic front camera with zero yaw, zero pitch, zero roll, and no perspective foreshortening. Use an evenly lit, seamless, shadowless light-neutral background with no ground plane, floor horizon, contact shadow, or cast shadow.
- Existing high-detail Human or Elf Shadowknight images may guide only realism, adult anatomy, facial quality, skin/material response, and restrained SoulDrifter material mood. Never copy or transfer their armor, clothing, class identity, weapons, effects, silhouette, or finished gear.
- A source image in an A-pose is a rejection. After a strict-T source becomes an accepted canonical mesh and rig, derive the required production A-pose by rotating that same skeleton. Never generate, purchase, or remodel a second body to obtain the A-pose.
